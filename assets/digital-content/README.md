# Percy Peptides digital content drafts

These are initial branded assets for customer review, prepared 20 October 2026 using Percy’s existing warm paper, forest green, sage, pale lime, and terracotta palette. The educational claims follow the current Percy research copy and must receive the client’s planned medical/legal/regulatory review before public use.

## Infographics

- `infographic-gpcr-signalling.png` — peptide ligand, GPCR signalling, experimental context, and a clinical-evidence caveat.
- `infographic-retatrutide-receptors.png` — GIPR, GLP-1R, and GCGR research targets with a formulation/context caveat.
- `infographic-mots-c-research.png` — mitochondrial 12S rRNA region, MOTS-c, research areas, and an evidence caveat.

PNG files are portrait 4:5 social/editorial drafts. Review the text and scientific diagram before publication.

## Educational animations

- `animation-gpcr-signal.svg` — a 10-second loop: ligand moves to the GPCR, three intracellular signal nodes appear in order, and the measurement curve draws last.
- `animation-retatrutide-targets.svg` — a 9-second loop: the GIPR, GLP-1R, and GCGR connection paths and labels are highlighted one at a time.

The SVGs are editable, self-contained, portrait 4:5 motion graphics. Movement now follows the scientific sequence and avoids decorative drifting or repeated pulsing. They play in modern browsers and show all stages without animation when reduced motion is requested. Confirm the client's desired delivery format before converting or publishing them.
