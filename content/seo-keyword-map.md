**Prepared:** 20 October 2026  
**Status:** Applied to the local prototype; UK targeting is provisional and client/regulatory review is still required.

## Research basis and limits

The current brief and copy are UK-facing, so this first pass assumes people searching from the UK. Public search results were reviewed for wording and intent. No Google Search Console, Keyword Planner, paid keyword database, or client analytics account was available; therefore this map contains no search-volume, difficulty, traffic, or ranking estimates. Validate those with Percy after the target market is confirmed and the production domain is live.

The visible search results for supplier and compound purchase queries include commercial listings and user-generated claims. Those are not evidence that Percy may lawfully offer a product or make a product claim. This map avoids treatment, weight-loss, dosing, and outcome terms. Percy must obtain market-specific regulatory review before publishing or promoting sales content. See [UK Government guidance on advertising medicines](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advertise-your-medicines).

Google says page titles should be unique, clear, concise, and accurate; it may use meta descriptions for snippets, while it ignores the `meta keywords` tag for indexing and ranking. Accordingly, keyword targets below are editorial planning notes and are used naturally in page copy, titles, and headings rather than inserted as a keyword tag. [Google metadata guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags) · [Google title and snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/appearance/good-titles-snippets)

## On-page work applied

- Added a unique, descriptive title and meta description to the homepage and each of the three article pages.
- Added Open Graph and X/Twitter title and description metadata for those four pages.
- Kept the article pages as separate URLs with descriptive headings and links from the homepage research library.
- Kept `admin.html` marked `noindex,nofollow` so the local management screen is not intended as a search landing page.
- Removed the homepage `meta keywords` tag. Its terms are retained as admin planning notes because Google does not use that tag for ranking.
