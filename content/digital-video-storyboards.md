## Video 1 — What does a receptor study measure?

**Length:** About 22 seconds  
**Audience:** General science-interested viewers  
**Narration:**

> When researchers study a peptide signal, binding is only one question. They may examine which receptor is involved, how the cell responds, and what the assay measures. Results depend on the receptor, cell type, and experimental conditions. A mechanism helps describe a research question; by itself, it does not establish a clinical outcome.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | A peptide marker moves toward a simple membrane diagram. | What does a receptor study measure? |
| 3–7 s | GPCR outline highlights; the ligand pauses at the receptor. | Receptor interaction |
| 7–12 s | A small signal path appears inside a stylised cell. | Cellular response |
| 12–17 s | A simple assay plate and measurement curve appear. | What the assay measures |
| 17–22 s | Context labels resolve, followed by the evidence note. | Receptor · cell type · assay · conditions / A mechanism is not a clinical outcome. |

**Audience:** General science-interested viewers
